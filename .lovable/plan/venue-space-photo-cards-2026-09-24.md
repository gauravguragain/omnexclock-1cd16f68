# Venue space photo cards

## What will change
- Replace the current venue-space table with separate visual cards, following the draft’s compact venue catalogue style.
- Give every venue card one large cover/profile photo, its name, capacity, supported layouts, description, monthly event count, and existing edit/archive controls.
- Open a photo gallery when the cover photo is selected, with previous/next navigation and thumbnail selection.
- Expand the add/edit venue window so staff can upload multiple photos, choose which one is the cover, preview them, and remove unwanted photos.
- Keep active, archived, and search filters so existing space management remains available on phones, tablets, and desktop.

## Data and access
- Add cover-photo and gallery-photo fields to venue spaces without changing existing venue records.
- Store venue photos in a dedicated private business-scoped storage area.
- Restrict viewing and photo management to users who already have Sales & Marketing access for that business.

## Validation
- Verify creating and editing a space, uploading several photos, changing the cover, opening the gallery, and archive/search behavior.
- Check the cards and gallery in both phone and desktop layouts, including dark mode.
