SUPER ADMIN

Welcome, Super Admin!

As a Super Admin, you have the highest level of access to your business on Pro Regal Pavilion. This guide covers everything you need to know to manage your business effectively.

Your Access & Responsibilities

You have full access to all features:

FeatureAccess
Dashboard & Analytics✅ Full access
Employee Management✅ Add, edit, deactivate employees
Roster Scheduling✅ Create, publish, manage rosters
Live Monitor✅ Real-time clock status
Timesheets✅ View, edit, approve timesheets
Payroll✅ Generate & manage payroll
Employee Requests✅ Approve/reject leave & availability
Forum / Noticeboard✅ Create posts & moderate
Audit Log✅ Full history
Inventory (FOH & Bar)✅ Full control — add items, stocktake, approve orders
User Management✅ Invite admins, assign roles, manage users
Business Settings✅ Logo, theme, configuration
Kiosk✅ Launch on-site kiosk
Exclusive to Super Admin: Only Super Admins can access User Management to invite new admins, assign roles (Admin, Viewer, Roster Admin), and remove users from the business. Regular Admins cannot access this feature.

Getting Started

Log in at the landing page by clicking "Business Admin"

Select your business from the Business Hub

You'll see the full sidebar navigation with all modules

Start by reviewing the Dashboard for a business overview

Inviting Team Members

One of your key responsibilities is managing who has access to the admin panel:

Navigate to User Management in the sidebar

Click "Invite Admin" in the top right

Enter the person's email address

Select their role: Super Admin, Admin, Viewer, or Roster Admin

For Roster Admin, select which departments they should manage

Click "Send Invitation" — they'll receive an email with signup instructions and their own induction guide

Key Modules Overview

Employees

Add and manage employee profiles with 4-digit codes, departments, job titles, and pay rates. Employees use their code to clock in at the Kiosk.

Roster

Create weekly rosters with shift assignments. Publish to make them visible to employees. Email notifications can be sent when publishing.

Kiosk

The on-site clock-in/out station. Launch from the sidebar — it requires employees to enter their code and take a photo.

Timesheets

Automatically generated from clock events. Review, edit, and approve timesheets before generating payroll.

Payroll

Generate payroll entries from approved timesheets. Export as CSV and email reports.

Inventory

Manage FOH and Bar stock with stocktakes, order requests, and auto-fulfillment. Add/delete items, approve orders, and export reports as CSV. See the Inventory Management Guide for details.

Security Best Practices

  • Only grant Super Admin access to trusted managers
  • Use strong, unique passwords
  • Regularly review user access in User Management
  • Remove access for departing team members immediately
  • Review the Audit Log periodically for unusual activity
Important: As Super Admin, you can modify all data and manage all users. Handle this access responsibly and ensure that your login credentials are secure.
