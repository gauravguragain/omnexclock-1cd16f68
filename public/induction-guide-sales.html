SALES & MARKETING

Welcome to Sales & Events

The Sales & Events workspace manages every enquiry from first contact to the finished event or catering job. Open it from the Business Hub. Access requires the Sales & Marketing role (or Super Admin).

All lists show the closest date first. Every screen updates live — no refresh needed. On mobile, use the menu button at the top or the bottom tabs.

The Pipeline

  1. New → Contacted → Inspection booked → Inspected → Deposit received → Confirmed stages → Full payment received → Completed
  2. Drag a lead card between columns, or open the lead and use the stage chips and quick actions.
  3. Declining a lead moves it to Cold and keeps the reason; reopening returns it to New.
  4. Tap anywhere on a lead tile to open its full-page editor (Details, Inspection, Menu selection, Run sheet tabs). Continue workflow opens the same editor at the next step.

Leads & Bookings

  • Record every call, email or meeting in the Interaction log — bullets and line breaks are kept as typed.
  • An event can use multiple venue spaces. Space names are matched to your venue spaces automatically.
  • Dropdowns with an Other option let you type your own value.
  • Optional menu tasting reminder creates a task at the chosen Sydney time.
  • Confirmed catering bookings can be fully edited (Details, Menu selection, Team & schedule).

Inspections & Tasks

  • Inspections have start and end times. Past inspections leave the upcoming list but stay saved.
  • Mark each as Complete, Reschedule (updates the calendar) or No show (flags follow-up).
  • Add manual tasks with due date, time, priority, notes and an optional lead.

Menus & Guest Selection

  • Build packages from dish courses (entrees veg/non-veg, mains, sides, desserts), live stalls (per person or flat rate) and corkage.
  • Dishes can require a protein choice. Hover a dish photo to enlarge; replace or delete it.
  • Send the client a guest selection link showing only the package dishes, with photos, dietary and allergy fields. When they submit, the lead's Menu selection tab updates automatically.

Run Sheets

  1. Open the lead's Run sheet tab and complete the readiness checklist.
  2. Choose salesperson, event coordinator and onsite contacts from your coordinator lists (phone numbers fill in).
  3. Review the compact A4 preview — live stalls with timings, food serving and FOH service schedules, guest counts (adults/kids), no pricing.
  4. Click Confirm & send (or Resend) to email the client confirmation and the kitchen/coordinator team a web run sheet link. Every revision is tracked.

Payments

Payments are tracked here only — invoices stay in Xero. Recording a payment automatically moves the lead: paid ≥ deposit → Deposit received; paid ≥ total → Full payment received.

Importing Data

  • Leads → Import: upload your client Excel sheet (client name, event date, event type, room, start/end time, deposit). A preview appears before anything is saved; re-importing updates rather than duplicates.
  • Settings → Import past events from iVvy: brings in historical bookings as Completed with their payment history. Anything with "Catering" in the booking name goes to catering.

Calendar Feed

Use the universal calendar (ICS) link in Settings to subscribe from Google, Outlook or Apple Calendar.
