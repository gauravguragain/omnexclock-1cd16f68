OWNER

Welcome to Operations

Operations is the owner's view of the whole business. It appears on the home screen for users with the Owner role (per business) and for Master accounts. It is view-only — nothing can be edited here.

Getting In

  1. On the home page click the Operations card and sign in.
  2. Owners go straight to their business's Operations dashboard; Master accounts choose a business first.

Date Ranges

Choose a preset or custom range. Events and catering are counted by their event/delivery date, not the date they were created. Revenue means booked value (not cash collected).

Calendar

View full calendar opens a full-page calendar of all events and catering (colour coded). Click any item for a read-only popup with the organiser's name and an email button.

Report Builder

  1. Pick one or more datasets (leads, bookings, payments, catering, staff hours, compliance and more).
  2. Choose the columns and date range.
  3. Export as Excel (one sheet per section with totals) or PDF. Save the setup as a template to reuse.
