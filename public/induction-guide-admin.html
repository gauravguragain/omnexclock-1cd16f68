Getting Started

Log in at the landing page by clicking "Business Admin"

Select your business from the Business Hub

You'll see the sidebar navigation with all available modules

Start with the Dashboard for a real-time overview

Daily Operations

1. Managing Employees

Navigate to Employees to add staff. Each employee gets a unique 4-digit code they use to clock in.

  • Set departments, job titles, and pay rates
  • Deactivate employees when they leave (don't delete — this preserves history)

2. Creating Rosters

Navigate to Roster to build weekly schedules:

  • Use the week navigator to select the week
  • Click cells to add/edit shifts for each employee
  • Set start/end times and break minutes
  • Click "Publish" to make shifts visible to employees
  • Optionally email roster notifications to employees

3. Running the Kiosk

Click "Launch Kiosk" in the sidebar to open the clock-in/out station on a shared device:

  • Employees enter their 4-digit code
  • Take a photo for verification
  • Clock in, start/end break, or clock out

4. Reviewing Timesheets

Timesheets are auto-generated from clock events. Navigate to Timesheets to:

  • Review daily clock-in/out times
  • Edit entries if corrections are needed
  • Approve timesheets for payroll processing

5. Processing Payroll

Navigate to Payroll after approving timesheets:

  • Generate payroll entries from approved timesheets
  • Review employee hours and pay calculations
  • Export as CSV or email reports
