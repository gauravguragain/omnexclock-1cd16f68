ROSTER ADMIN

Welcome, Roster Admin!

As a Roster Admin, you manage rosters and timesheets for your assigned departments. This focused role lets you handle scheduling without needing full admin access.

Your Access

FeatureAccess
Roster Scheduling✅ Create, edit, publish shifts for your departments
Timesheets✅ View and approve timesheets for your departments
Employee List (your depts)✅ View employees in your departments
Dashboard❌ Not available
Inventory (FOH & Bar)✅ View, stocktake, submit order requests
Payroll❌ Not available
Live Monitor❌ Not available
User Management❌ Not available
Business Settings❌ Not available
Department Scope: You can only see and manage employees and shifts within your assigned departments. Your Super Admin determines which departments you have access to.

Getting Started

Log in at the landing page by clicking "Business Admin"

Select your business from the Business Hub

You'll see Roster and Timesheets in your sidebar

Your assigned departments will be shown in the header badge

Managing Rosters

Navigate to Roster

Use the week navigator to select the target week

You'll see employees from your assigned departments only

Click on cells to add or edit shifts

Set start time, end time, and break minutes for each shift

Click "Publish" to make shifts visible to employees

Optionally use "Email All" to notify employees of their roster

Tips for Roster Management

  • Draft shifts are only visible to admins — employees see only published shifts
  • You can copy shifts from the previous week to save time
  • Use the notes field for special instructions

Managing Timesheets

Navigate to Timesheets

Select the date range to review

Review clock-in/out times for your department employees

Edit entries if corrections are needed (e.g., missed clock-out)

Approve timesheets when they're accurate

Important: Once a timesheet is approved, it can be used to generate payroll. Double-check all entries before approving.

Inventory

As a Roster Admin, you can manage inventory stock levels:

  • View all FOH and Bar inventory items and order history
  • Perform weekly stocktakes to update counts
  • Submit order requests for items that need replenishment
  • Export inventory data and stocktake reports as CSV
Note: You cannot add/delete items or approve/reject orders — those actions are reserved for Admins and Super Admins.

For the full guide, see the Inventory Management Guide.

Need Help?

Contact your Super Admin or business administrator for assistance, to request access to additional departments, or to change your access level.
