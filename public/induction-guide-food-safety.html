FOOD SAFETY

Welcome to Food Safety Logs

Food Safety Logs is the HACCP record-keeping module, found inside the business admin panel. It is visible to users with the Food Safety Manager role (or Super Admin). This role can be combined with other roles on the same person.

How It Works

  • Every log is a configurable form — temperature checks, checklists, cleaning records and receiving logs. There are no hard-coded forms.
  • Six starter forms are provided. Edit them, add your own, or upload an Excel template and adjust it in the form editor.
  • Readings outside the allowed range raise an alert to the Food Safety Manager by email.

Staff Logging

  1. Print the QR code from Food Safety settings and place it in the kitchen.
  2. Staff scan it (or open /logs) and enter their 4-digit staff code on the keypad.
  3. They choose a form, fill it in and submit. Logging works offline and syncs when the connection returns.

Manager Tasks

  • Review submitted entries and follow up on alerts.
  • Export records to Excel for inspections and audits.
  • Archive or permanently delete forms you no longer use.
