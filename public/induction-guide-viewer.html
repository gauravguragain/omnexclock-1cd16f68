Getting Started

Log in at the landing page by clicking "Business Admin"

Select your business from the Business Hub

Browse any module from the sidebar to view data

Your Role: As a Viewer, you are ideal for oversight, auditing, or monitoring purposes. You can see all operational data including employee schedules, clock events, timesheets, and payroll — but all buttons and edit functions will be disabled.

What You Can Monitor

  • Dashboard — Real-time business metrics and charts
  • Employees — Staff profiles, departments, and contact details
  • Roster — Weekly shift schedules for all employees
  • Live Monitor — Who is currently clocked in, on break, or out
  • Timesheets — Daily clock-in/out records and hours worked
  • Payroll — Pay calculations and payment status
  • Audit Log — Full history of all actions taken on the platform

Need Help?

Contact your Super Admin or business administrator for assistance or to request a different access level.
