1. Platform Overview

Regal Clock is a cloud-based workforce management platform designed for small-to-medium businesses. It provides:

  • Time Tracking — Clock in/out with photo verification via an on-site Kiosk
  • Roster Scheduling — Create, publish, and manage weekly employee schedules
  • Timesheet Management — Automatic timesheet generation from clock events with approval workflows
  • Payroll Calculation — Generate payroll entries based on approved timesheets
  • Employee Self-Service — Employees can view rosters, timesheets, submit leave requests, and participate in forums via their own portal
  • Multi-Business Support — A single platform supporting multiple businesses with isolated data

4. Registering a New Business

To register a new business on the platform:

Click "Register Business" on the landing page

If you don't have an account, create one by entering your full name, email, and password

Verify your email address by clicking the confirmation link sent to your inbox

Return to the registration page and sign in

Enter your Business Name (e.g., "Pro Regal Pavilion")

Enter a unique Business Code (e.g., "PRP") — this is used in all URLs and by employees

Click Register Business

Important: The business code must be at least 3 characters. It will be converted to uppercase. Choose something short and memorable as employees will use it to access their portal.

After registration, you become the Admin of that business and are redirected to the Admin Dashboard.

5. Business Admin Login

9. Kiosk – Clock In/Out

The Kiosk is a dedicated time-clock interface designed for on-site use (e.g., a tablet at the front desk).

Accessing the Kiosk

The Kiosk URL for your business is:

/b/YOUR_CODE/kiosk

For example, Pro Regal Pavilion's Kiosk is at: /b/PRP/kiosk

How Employees Use the Kiosk

The Kiosk displays the business name, logo, and current time

Employee enters their 4-digit code using the on-screen numpad

The system shows the employee's name and current status (Clocked Out / Clocked In / On Break)

Available actions are shown based on status:

  • Clock In — Available when clocked out
  • Start Break — Available when clocked in
  • End Break — Available when on break
  • Clock Out — Available when clocked in or on break

Employee selects an action and takes a photo (selfie) for verification

A confirmation message appears, and the Kiosk resets for the next employee

Important: The Kiosk requires camera access. Ensure the device's browser has camera permissions enabled.

Admin Login from Kiosk

There is an "Admin Login" button on the Kiosk page. Clicking it will:

  1. Sign out any existing admin session (for security)
  2. Redirect to the business admin login page

This ensures the Kiosk cannot be used to access a previously logged-in admin session.

14b. Inventory Management

Navigate to Inventory in the sidebar to manage stock levels across two sections: FOH (Front of House) and Bar.

How It Works

  • Each section has its own items, categories, and order tracking
  • Items have a Par Level (minimum stock target) — items below par show a "Low Stock" alert
  • Stock levels are updated primarily through weekly stocktakes
  • Order requests follow a workflow: Pending → Approved → Ordered → Received
  • When a stocktake shows an item has reached par level, "Ordered" requests are automatically marked as received

Key Actions

ActionWho Can Do It
View items & ordersAll roles
Perform stocktakeAdmin, Super Admin, Roster Admin
Submit order requestsAdmin, Super Admin, Roster Admin
Add/delete itemsAdmin, Super Admin
Approve/reject/mark orderedAdmin, Super Admin

For the complete Inventory guide, see the Inventory Management Guide.

15. Audit Log

The Audit Log records all significant actions taken within the business:

  • User sign-ins
  • Employee additions and edits
  • Timesheet edits, approvals, and unapprovals
  • Shift changes
  • Request approvals/rejections
  • All admin actions are timestamped with the performing user's ID
Compliance: The audit log cannot be edited or deleted. It provides a complete, tamper-proof record of all business operations.

20. Security & Best Practices

For Admins

  • Use a strong, unique password (minimum 6 characters, recommended 12+)
  • Never share your admin credentials
  • Regularly review the Audit Log for suspicious activity
  • Revoke access for employees or admins who no longer need it
  • Log out after each session, especially on shared devices

For Kiosk Setup

  • Use a dedicated tablet for the Kiosk
  • Enable Guided Access (iOS) or Kiosk Mode (Android/Chrome) to lock the browser to the Kiosk page
  • Ensure the tablet has a front-facing camera for photo verification
  • Position the tablet at eye level near the workplace entrance

For Employees

  • Keep your 4-digit employee code confidential
  • Report any suspected misuse to your manager immediately
  • Always clock in/out honestly — photo verification is stored

21. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: An employee forgot to clock out. What do I do?

Go to Timesheets, find the employee's entry for that day, and manually edit the clock-out time. The change will be recorded in the Audit Log.

Q: How do I give someone read-only access to my business?

Go to User Management and grant them the Viewer role. They can see all pages but cannot make any changes.

Q: Can an employee work for two businesses?

Yes. Each business has separate employee records. The employee will have a different 4-digit code for each business. They can use the "Change Business" button on the portal to switch.

Q: What happens if the internet goes down at the Kiosk?

The Kiosk requires an internet connection to record clock events. If the connection is down, employees should note their times manually, and an admin can enter them via the Timesheets page later.

Q: How do I change my business theme?

Go to My Business in the admin panel. Upload a logo, and click "Analyse Theme" to get AI-suggested colours, or manually set your preferred palette.

Q: Can I export data?

Yes. Employee lists, timesheets, and payroll can be exported as CSV files using the export button on each respective page.
